Event Timeline

5 March 2025

Android Workshop 2025

1:30 PM – 4:30 PM  ·  Computer Lab, AB4

Event Flow

The session opened with a welcome address by the branch chairperson, followed by an introduction to Android development fundamentals. Senior student mentors led hands-on live-coding demos where participants built a simple app from scratch. The workshop covered UI design, the Activity lifecycle, and basic API calls, closing with a Q&A and certificate distribution. Limited to 50 registered participants.

29 March 2025

E-Symposium

Full-day event  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

The E-Symposium brought together students and researchers to present cutting-edge ideas across electronics, AI, and sustainable engineering. The day began with a keynote address, followed by parallel technical paper presentations evaluated by faculty judges. Lightning talks and live project demonstrations filled the afternoon, and the event concluded with an awards ceremony honouring top presenters and an open networking session.

13–14 May 2025 · Zealicon 2025

Checkmate Challenge

Zealicon 2025 Fest  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

A strategic chess tournament drawing participants from across the university as part of Zealicon 2025. The competition used a Swiss-system format across five rounds with rapid time control (15+10). Opening and closing ceremonies were hosted by the branch chairperson, and a simultaneous exhibition game by a senior club member added excitement. The top three finalists received trophies and IEEE membership vouchers.

13–14 May 2025 · Zealicon 2025

BotBlitz Robotics Competition

AB-2 Ground Floor  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

BotBlitz was a head-to-head robotics challenge held during the university's flagship fest Zealicon 2025. Teams of up to three participants assembled and programmed bots to navigate obstacle courses and complete timed challenges. Preliminary heats ran in the morning, followed by semi-finals and a grand finale in the evening. The top three teams received trophies and IEEE-branded merchandise.

14 May 2025 · Zealicon 2025

Poster Presentation Competition

Zealicon 2025 Fest  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

Students showcased their research ideas through scientific posters covering electronics, computer science, and green technology during Zealicon 2025. Each team presented to a panel of faculty and industry evaluators during a dedicated judging window. Posters were assessed on novelty, clarity, and technical depth. The event closed with an open gallery walk for all attendees before prize distribution.

8 October 2025

IEEE Membership Drive & Initiation Ceremony

1:45 PM – 2:45 PM  ·  Multipurpose Hall (MPH), JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

The event opened with a formal initiation ceremony welcoming new IEEE members and officially launching the IEEE Blockchain Community within the branch. Branch Counsellor Dr. Nitima Malsa addressed the gathering on the benefits of IEEE membership. New members took the IEEE pledge, received membership kits, and were introduced to the executive council. The ceremony closed with the announcement of sub-chapter leadership and an informal mixer.

October 2025 · IEEE Day

IEEE Day 2025 – Technical Talk

IEEE Day Celebrations  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

As part of global IEEE Day celebrations, the branch hosted an expert talk titled "Building Scalable Applications — What Startups Can Teach You." The speaker walked attendees through real-world startup architectures, microservices design patterns, and lessons learned at scale. The session included a live demo, an interactive Q&A, and downloadable resource links. Participants received IEEE Day participation certificates.

October 2025 · IEEE Day

IEEE Day 2025 – Technical Session

IEEE Day Celebrations  ·  JSS University, Noida

Event Flow

The second IEEE Day session, "Innovation Through Prototyping — From Fab Lab to Market," focused on turning ideas into market-ready products. The speaker guided participants through rapid prototyping techniques, Fab Lab tools (laser cutters, 3D printers, CNC), and the business side of hardware startups. A hands-on show-and-tell segment let attendees interact with prototype examples, followed by a panel discussion with branch alumni working in product companies.
