About Us

IEEE JSS University Student Branch - Noida

IEEE JSS University Student Branch - Noida is the official IEEE Student Branch of JSS University, Noida, established on 10 February 2025, operating under the IEEE Uttar Pradesh Section (Region 10 – Asia Pacific) with Student Branch Code STB60228176 and School Code SCH60228176. Although formally inaugurated in February 2025, the journey began in October 2024 with 15 founding members, and the branch has now grown to around 90 active student members across multiple engineering disciplines.

Learn More

Our Mission & Vision

Our Vision

To cultivate a culture of technological innovation, research excellence, and professional leadership among students by connecting them with global IEEE opportunities and empowering them to develop impactful solutions for society.

Our Mission

  • To promote technical knowledge through workshops, seminars, and hands-on learning initiatives
  • To encourage research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • To develop leadership and professional competencies among students
  • To connect students with global IEEE communities and opportunities
  • To contribute to societal development through technology-driven solutions

Benefits of Joining IEEE JSS University Student Branch

Professional Development

Get access to various resources, tools, and opportunities that help you grow professionally and advance your career.

Networking

Connect with like-minded individuals, industry professionals, and experts in your field.

Learning

Participate in workshops, seminars, and events that help you learn new skills and stay updated with the latest trends in technology.

Community

Be a part of a global community of IEEE members and volunteers who are passionate about technology and innovation.

How to Join

Students from all academic disciplines can apply via the Join Now form, or connect with any Executive Team member or faculty coordinator for guidance. The branch conducts 2-3 membership drives each year — follow our social media for updates.

ICSETI 2025 Conference Collaboration

The branch served as a collaborating partner for the International Conference on Sustainable Engineering and Technology Innovations (ICSETI 2025), held 5th-6th February at JSS University, Noida — featuring 8+ technical sessions and competitions. IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group

The IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Affinity Group at JSS University Noida works toward promoting diversity, inclusion, and empowerment of women in engineering and technology through leadership initiatives, mentorship, and technical engagement.

The WIE team has conducted multiple initiatives aimed at encouraging participation, professional growth, and leadership development among students, particularly women in STEM fields.
